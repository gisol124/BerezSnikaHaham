The Smart Fire Hydrant

A hydrant that detects fire, calls 102 and starts fighting the flames before the firefighters even leave the station.

Learn more See the data

How it works

👁️
1. Detect

The hydrant is always on and looks for an unwanted flame.

📞
2. Report

It reports the fire to the 102 call center immediately.

💧
3. Extinguish

It sprays water to put out or slow the fire until help arrives.

Why do we need it?

9-14 min

Average time for firefighters to arrive in Israel

300-450°C

Temperature at which dry wood ignites

102

The fire emergency number we call automatically
